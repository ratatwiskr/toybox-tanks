## Goal

Make map authoring simpler by removing the manually authored outer `#` border from
map grids and automatically adding a solid one-cell border outside the defined
playable area. This is a behavior-preserving map-format refactor: the rendered and
collision arena, room order, spawns, enemies, and gameplay rules remain unchanged.

## Background and references

Read `AGENTS.md`, `docs/progress.md`, `docs/verification.md`, and
`docs/architecture.md` before editing. Inspect the current `main` revision and
existing tests before changing anything.

- `src/sim/maps.ts` currently contains three 24 × 13 grids that explicitly include
  a closed `#` outer border. `validateMap` enforces fixed dimensions, the closed
  border, spawn/sentry counts, and reachability from player 1.
- `src/sim/world.ts` converts `#` cells into `Wall` objects and loads players and
  sentries.
- `src/sim/collision.ts` uses `world.walls` for tank movement, swept shell
  collision, and sentry line of sight.
- `src/config.ts` defines the logical arena and tile geometry; verify the exact
  current constants before implementation.
- Preserve `reference/toybox-tanks.original.html` unchanged.

## Desired outcome

- Authored map grids represent only the 22 × 11 interior playable area.
- The map loader automatically adds a closed one-cell solid-wall border immediately
  outside that interior, producing the same 24 × 13 logical arena and identical
  wall geometry as today.
- The three existing rooms retain identical gameplay geometry, names, hints,
  expected enemy counts, spawn locations, sentry locations, and room order. Only
  the authored grid representation changes: remove the manually written outer
  border rows and columns.
- `validateMap` validates the 22 × 11 interior: dimensions, allowed symbols,
  spawn/sentry counts, and reachability from player 1. It no longer requires
  authors to draw the border. Do not weaken existing validation.
- Invalid maps continue to produce stable, actionable errors rather than rendering
  crashes.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] All three existing maps are represented as 22 × 11 interior grids without
      manually authored border cells.
- [ ] After loading, each room has exactly the same wall rectangles as before the
      change; compare authoritative wall geometry in focused tests.
- [ ] Player spawn positions, enemy positions/IDs/order, room names, hints,
      expected enemy counts, and room order remain unchanged.
- [ ] `validateMap` rejects incorrect interior dimensions, unknown symbols,
      missing or duplicate spawns, incorrect sentry counts, and unreachable
      playable area with helpful error messages.
- [ ] A tile on the outermost authored row or column is treated as interior and
      receives an automatically added solid wall outside it; no walkable gap exists
      at the arena edge.
- [ ] Unit tests cover auto-border geometry for all three rooms, validation
      errors, reachability, and world-load equivalence.
- [ ] Existing gameplay behavior is unchanged: one wall bounce, swept collision,
      solo/co-op entity counts, outcome rules, and reset behavior.

## Non-goals and STOP conditions

- No cork, new rooms, map editor, runtime fetching, engine/framework change, or
  change to collision rules, arena size, or the original HTML reference.
- Do not change room order, spawn semantics, enemy counts, or gameplay rules.
- STOP and ask for an owner decision if preserving identical wall geometry would
  require changing `CONFIG`, room order, spawns, or gameplay behavior.
